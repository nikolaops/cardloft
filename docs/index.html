Everything you need, nothing you don't

Plan side projects, client work or the house renovation in the same place, without the weight of an enterprise tracker.

Kanban board

Drag cards between your own columns. Rename, reorder and color them to match how you work.

Sprints & backlog

Keep a backlog of ideas and pull the right tickets into a sprint with its own goal and dates.

Rich tickets

Descriptions, private notes, priorities, colored labels and comments on every ticket.

Separate projects

Switch between fully separated projects from the sidebar, each with its own color and emoji.

Sticky notes

A notebook per project for meeting notes, ideas and checklists, on colored sticky cards.

Light & dark

Follows your system appearance and feels at home on both macOS and Windows.

Private by design

Cardloft never talks to a server. Your whole workspace is one JSON file you can back up, sync or delete like any other file.

  • Works fully offline — on a plane, behind a firewall, anywhere
  • No account, no telemetry, no subscription

Install in a minute

Cardloft is a small independent app and isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so your system asks once before opening it.

macOS

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Cardloft into Applications.
  2. Open Cardloft. When macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

macOS 13 Ventura or newer · Apple silicon and Intel

Download for macOS

Windows

  1. Unzip the download to a folder of your choice.
  2. Run Cardloft.exe. If SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.

Windows 10 or 11 · uses the built-in Microsoft Edge WebView2
